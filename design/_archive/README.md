# Archive

Retired logo explorations and the v1 look (cream paper, amber accent, Fraunces, Inter, the threshold mark),
kept as a record only. Nothing here is used by the app or by the brand files.

- `logo/`: the first 26 directions and their radar studies.
- `final/`: the "six final ideas" board.
- `studio-b/`: Studio B's three directions.
- `brand/palettes-v1.json`: the v1 colour exploration (five palettes, cream paper and amber among them), moved out of `design/brand/`.
- `studio-a/`: Studio A's three directions (01 Swing, 02 Coupe, 03 Ola), their pixel masters and the deck.

The shipped identity is Marquise: `design/brand/` and the Swing final masters in `design/studio-a/`
(`swing-final.mjs` generates every one of them).
