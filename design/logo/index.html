Turnout  ·  logo directions  ·  six thoughts, two minutes

Six ways to draw people showing up.

Each direction is one idea for a hospitality-plus-data brand on warm paper: a door, a turn, a count, a light, a crowd, a letter. For each: the hero lockup, the mark at 16 to 64 px, the favicon in a tab, the real app header at 20 px, and one honest line.

Recommendation

Build on the threshold. Keep the turn if you want quieter. The window is the icon.

1. The threshold

It is the owner's idea, finished, and it passes every test on this sheet: 16 px favicon, 20 px header, one colour, reverse. The floor of the door is the baseline of the word. Ship it.

2. The turn

The most elegant drawing and the one that says the name. It reads at every size with nothing to simplify. Choose it if the brand should feel like a considered hospitality house rather than a tool; the threshold can stay as the icon language inside the product.

3. The lit window

The most emotional: a place with one light on. It is the best app icon of the six and the weakest in the 20 px header, so it would live as the icon, with the wordmark alone in the header.

The count (03) is honest and strong in one colour but has no place in it; it is a chart, not a door. The crowd (05) tells the full story and fails the header test because it is wide and low. Under the n (06) is beautiful at 56 px and almost invisible at 20 px; it is a typographic detail worth keeping in the brand, not the mark to build on.
