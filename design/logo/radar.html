A  Five radar directions

Concrete, not abstract: a sweep, a radius with a door, a street corner, a beacon, and the arena-to-bar path. Same strokes (2.4 of 32, 2 px at 16), same wordmark, same honesty line.

B  Primary colour

Each palette shows the threshold (01) and venue-and-bar (11) lockups, a mini app header, a radar card, the three buttons, the WCAG ratios of the accent as text, and one line each on what it says and what it risks. Warm paper stays unless the palette genuinely needs a cooler neutral (P3).

Recommendation

Pairings, ranked

1. Threshold x The blend (01 x P4)

The approved mark, now in indigo-navy ink with a blue-violet signal and amber kept for hot venues. It reads as serious software to an investor and as a warm room to an operator, and it quietly says Qloo and Ticketmaster without wearing either logo. This is what I would ship for the jury.

2. Venue and bar x Pro blue (11 x P3)

The product story in one glyph, on the palette that looks most like live-events infrastructure. The most 'professional' combination on the sheet, at the cost of the warmth the owner asked for; keep it as the pitch-deck alternate.

3. Threshold x Bottle green (01 x P5)

Premium and warm: the hotel-bar palette. Distinctive in a room full of blue SaaS, with amber still lighting the radar. The ok colour needs a rethink, which is why it is third.

For the jury (an angel investor, a CTO, a board member, an entertainer, a Live Nation executive): ship 01 Threshold in P4, the blend. Navy ink and a blue-violet accent read as a serious, fundable tool to the investor and the CTO; the warm paper and the doorway read as hospitality to the entertainer and the operator; amber on the radar's hot venues keeps the product screen alive for the Live Nation executive, whose world that map is. Keep 11 Venue and bar as the explanatory glyph in the pitch (how it works), not as the logo.

Among the radar marks alone: 11 tells the product story, 10 is the cleanest and the most generic, 07 is the most beautiful at size and needs its tint, 09 is the most concrete and the heaviest, 08 is the richest and fails the favicon as drawn.
