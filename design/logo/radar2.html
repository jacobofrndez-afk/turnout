A real radar. One blip, the crowd detected.

Five variants of a true radar, immediately recognisable, with at most one blip in accent plus the centre: no dot fields, no clusters. Each is shown in the current palette (P1) and, directly beneath, in the front-runner P4 (navy ink #1B1F3B, blue-violet accent #4A56E2, warm paper). Strokes 2.4 of 32, 2 px at 16 px; thin rings 1.8; the sweep tint is the accent at 18 % so the palette swap is one token.

Recommendation

Top two

1. Half radar (14)

The only variant that is a radar and a good neighbour: 2:1, it sits beside the 20 px wordmark at the height of the serif without a ring fighting the letters, the blip stays 2.2 px, and at 16 px it keeps horizon, arc and blip. In P4 the navy horizon reads like a rule under the name.

2. Classic (12)

The most recognisable radar on the sheet; tint plus one blip keeps it civil. It loses a ring and the edge line at 16 px and its rings are soft there, which is why it is second. It is the one to use large, on the radar page itself.

Tomorrow, in the app header: Half radar (14) in P4, 20 px tall beside the 20 px wordmark, with Classic (12) reserved for the radar page's empty state and the app icon.

Rings and door (13) is the richest but carries two ideas; Sweep only (15) is beautiful and not yet a radar without a ring; Radar in the o (16) survives 16 px honestly as a favicon but loses its blip in the 20 px word.
