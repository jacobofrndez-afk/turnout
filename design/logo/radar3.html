Classic, refined. Less childish, no fantasy.

Four executions of the chosen idea (two rings, a tinted sweep, one blip, a centre) with the restraint of a watch dial: hairlines, hierarchy between rings, a small blip at 43 degrees from vertical so it reads detected rather than pinned, tint opacity as a token. Each is shown at 96 px on paper, in P1 and in P4. Header rule: nothing renders under 1 px at 20 px, so 32-unit strokes below 1.5 snap to 1.5 there; each line says what else changes.

Anglessweep from 12 o'clock to 50 degrees from vertical · blip at 43 degrees
Tintaccent at 12 to 18 %, token --sweep-tint
16 pxone ring at 1 px (anti-aliased, as all circles), tint, blip r 2.6
Recommendation

Top one: Weighted (18)

It keeps the hierarchy at every size, has nothing that disappears, and puts the eye on the blip, which is the product's promise. Dial (17) is finer at 96 px but flattens at 20 px; Open ring (19) is the cleverest and risks the spinner; Lens (20) is the most editorial and loses its third ring below 48 px.
