Weighted, with the afterglow of a real radar.

Three executions of 18 Weighted with the sweep as a trail that fades behind the leading edge instead of a flat sector. Rings refined and even. The trail is 14 stacked sectors with stepped opacity (SVG has no conic gradient); the steps are invisible from 24 px up, and at 16 px the favicon falls back to one flat 60 degree sector at 18 %. Blip at 43 degrees from vertical on the inner ring, inside the brightest part of the trail. Each shown at 96 px, in P1 and in P4.

Tokens--sweep-tint (max opacity) · --ring-outer, --ring-inner (weights) · --mark-ink-soft (23 only)
One colourtrail in ink at 0.6 x, blip ink
Reverserings paper, trail x1.5, blip accent
Recommendation

Top one: Afterglow (21)

Ninety degrees is enough to read as a sweep and short enough to stay out of the way of the wordmark; the rings keep their hierarchy; nothing in it depends on a hairline. 22 is livelier and loses its edge at 24 px; 23 is the most beautiful at 96 px and the softest at 20 px.
