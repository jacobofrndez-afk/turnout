The screen. Rings, a line, an afterglow. No dots.

Three executions of the classic radar screen: thin concentric rings, a crisp radius from the centre to the rim as the sweep's leading edge, a wide afterglow fading behind it. Nothing at the centre, no blip. The edge line starts exactly at the centre and ends on the inner side of the outer ring. Strokes snap to 1.5 / 1.2 at header size; at 16 px: one ring at 2 units, the line at 2, a flat 60 degree glow at 0.2. Each at 96 px, in P1 and in P4.

Edge50 degrees from vertical, butt caps, centre to r 13.5
One colouredge and trail in ink, trail x0.6
Reverserings paper · edge: accent in 24, paper in 25 and 26 · trail x1.5
Recommendation

Top one: Screen (24)

Two rings, one accent line, one glow: the fewest parts that are unmistakably a radar, and the accent is the sweep itself, so the brand's one colour means 'now'. 25 is the richest and loses its ticks and third ring small; 26 is the quietest and its accent nearly disappears on white under 24 px.
