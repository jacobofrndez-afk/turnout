Swing, final

The needle pivots on a round hub inlaid in the end of the left wall: the hub is tangent to the wall's top, bottom and end, and the wall end follows the hub's own circle, so the wine hub sits in the aubergine wall with nothing between them, no paper reveal, no hairline. Down the needle's left edge, round the hub, then square down to the floor: the wall's lower lip ends in a face, never a sliver tapering into the circle's tangent with the floor. Under the cut a collar of wine, its own path hidden by the ink, fills whatever the cut leaves, so the page never shows through the joint, and the needle path stays exactly what it was. Where the hub is small (6 px across at 128, 12 at 256, 8 in the 180 master) the cut is hinted to whole pixels: every pixel of the joint is wine or ink, never a blend, and the hub reads as a crisp round pixel outline. That hinting is for 1x screens: on a 2x screen the component draws the true curve instead, so the hub never sits in a doubled staircase. One colour and reverse draw the same joint: in one colour hub and wall simply close into one ink. Below 128 px the hub becomes a foot of whole pixels at the wall end with its outer corner rounded, also with no clearance. The outer arc is full ink at two thirds of the walls' weight from 32 px up (4 px at 128), so mark and word carry the same weight; at 20 and 16 px it is one device pixel of ink at 65 %. No gradient and no hairline: the afterglow is one flat tint, the door's swept area, and the inner ring is gone. Colours are Velours: walls, arc and word aubergine #3B1C42, needle wine #84204D, afterglow #F0E4EA, on the white page; reversed on the aubergine plate #3B1C42 with #F6F1F8, dusty rose #E6A4C2 and the plum tint #5A3459. Personality: live. The mark is still; on the home hero the needle swings open once, 900 ms, the afterglow trailing behind it.
