# Turnout, two-minute demo script

For judges and for the submission video. Everything below runs on the live app; nothing is pre-recorded.

## 0:00 Home

"Turn the crowd outside into customers inside." The card on the right is live: the biggest crowd near Penn & Pour, a bar two blocks from Madison Square Garden, in the next 48 hours.

Click **Try Penn & Pour · New York**.

## 0:15 Radar

Real Ticketmaster events within walking distance, scored by crowd size, distance and timing. Tours, exhibitions and add-on tickets are filtered out. The ring is the score; amber cards are worth a campaign.

Click **Qualify this crowd with Qloo** on the top event.

## 0:40 The crowd, through Qloo

Point at four things:

1. the resolved entity (auto-matched, or the "which one is playing tonight?" picker when the name is ambiguous);
2. age and gender affinity, positive means over-index;
3. the bars within 1.5 km this crowd already likes, with affinity;
4. the merchant's own offers ranked against this crowd.

Open **Provenance** at the bottom: every Qloo call, live or cached, with status and result count.

Click **Draft the campaign →**.

## 1:10 The campaign

The chosen offer with the rationale citing Qloo evidence by name. Say the line: "the sports-bar offer had the highest raw score; the agent picked the cocktail happy hour because this crowd skews women 25 to 34 and over-indexes on rooftop cocktail bars and luxury brands."

Scroll: geofenced ad with interests derived from Qloo tags, Google post, WhatsApp to regulars, chalkboard and staff line, screen. Then **What this does not establish**.

Click **Approve and publish →**. The Published block lists one send per channel with its status: real (WhatsApp on the judge's phone when the sandbox is joined), created-paused (Snapchat when an ad account is wired) or simulated with the exact payload.

## 1:45 Same bar, different night

Back to the radar, pick a Knicks or Rangers game. Same bar, same radius, same code: different demographics, different bars, different offer, different copy.

## 2:00 The everyday flow

**Neighbourhood pulse**: no event, just the street. Who walks past on a weekday, what they like in a place, the competitive set, and this week's board line and Google post.

## If asked

- Paris works too: Le Quai 12 at Accor Arena, campaign in French.
- Set up your own place at `/start`: address, offers in plain words, Qloo tags confirmed in one screen.
- Qloo describes groups, never individuals; no personal data is sent; raw Qloo data is never published.
