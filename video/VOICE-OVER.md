# Turnout demo, voice-over script (about 95 seconds)

Read calmly, like telling a friend who owns a bar. One idea per line. The on-screen captions
carry the same words, so the voice and the subtitles match. Lines marked (*) quote the day's
real data and are rewritten for the final take on the deployed app.

| Time | Line |
|---|---|
| 0:00 | Tonight, twenty thousand people walk out of Madison Square Garden. (*) |
| 0:04 | Two blocks from your bar. (*) |
| 0:07 | Turnout watches what happens around your place, all week. |
| 0:12 | Concerts, games, theatre crowds, the rhythm of your street. |
| 0:18 | Today, it picks the one that matters tonight. A real listing, a real time, a short walk from your door. |
| 0:28 | Then it asks Qloo what this crowd likes, and checks it against your street. |
| 0:35 | From your own menu, it picks the offer that fits. Tonight, happy hour beats the usual beer flight. (*) |
| 0:44 | It writes the campaign. You put your words on it, and the board rewrites itself. |
| 0:53 | You approve. Nothing goes out without you. |
| 0:58 | Chalkboard, WhatsApp to your regulars, a local ad. Prepared, not sent, nothing charged. |
| 1:07 | Your board, by the door, tonight. |
| 1:16 | Paris or New York. Any street with a crowd. |
| 1:22 | Turn the crowd outside into customers inside. |
| 1:27 | Prepared by Claude. Informed by Qloo. Your call. |

Recording: Voice Memos on the phone or QuickTime on the Mac, a quiet room, 20 cm from the mic,
one take per line is fine. Send the file; the cut is retimed to the voice and mixed with ffmpeg.
